Pulls the raw access event log via GET /api/v1/review?categoryFilter=Access through your local proxy (proxy.py running on the Pi). "Today" resets automatically at local midnight.

Doors & locations

"Door name" must match the raw What field from the Inception event log exactly (use Test Connection to check). "Display name" and "Location" are what staff see on the dashboard. Toggle = alternates In/Out (your main Entry Door — exit is only ever logged here). Entry only = only logs arrival at that location (exit unlogged); staff badging here are marked In at that Location, but only the toggle door can mark someone Out.
